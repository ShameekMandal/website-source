> **Application of Power K Means for Multiview Data** | Master's Final Year Project | Supervisor - Prof. Swagatam Das, ISI Kolkata  

Formulated a novel clustering framework extending Multiview Kernel Power K-Means to optimize non-convex objective functions and mitigate local minima limits. 

*   **Framework:** Kernelized data projections via Reproducing Kernel Hilbert Space (RKHS).
*   **Optimization:** Solved non-convexity constraints utilizing the Majorization-Minimization (MM) algorithm to dynamically weight inner clusters across multiple views.
*   **Benchmarks:** Validated clustering accuracy on simulated frameworks and Caltech 101 using NMI and ARI metrics.
